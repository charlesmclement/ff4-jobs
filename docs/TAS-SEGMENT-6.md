## Route

1. Heal, walk to the harbor, voyage. Walk the beach to Mysidia without talking to anyone (the guide warns they turn Cecil into a pig or toad).
2. Elder: the twins join. Shopping (guide):
   * Weapons: Flame Rod and Healing Staff.
   * Armor: 2 Gaia Gear, 2 Wizard's Hats, 2 Silver Armlets.
   * Items: 4 Tents, 10 Potions, 5 Phoenix Downs.
   * Strongest on the twins.
3. Walk 105 steps east to Mt. Ordeals. **Twins' grind:** loop (20,30)↔(16,31) just inside the entrance until Palom and Porom reach 12 (Palom learns Fira at 12, per the learning table). Rest early when Porom has under 6 MP or Palom under 15. Rests walk out, use a Tent and walk back in. Up to 3 blocks; blocks 2–3 are skipped once the levels are reached.
4. Fire event → ridge (Tellah) → formation: Palom, Cecil, Porom, (empty), Tellah, with only Cecil in front → pass → summit.
5. Summit: Tent and heal at the save point (28,13) → Milon → back to the save point, Tent and heal → Milon Z → mirror and trial → Strongest on everyone.
6. Walk down and out, Tent, walk back to Mysidia. The Elder sets flag 29.
7. Armor shop, cheapest first: Light Shield, Gauntlets, Light Helm, Knight's Armor. Strongest on everyone, item restock, out the south gate.

## Battle plans (guide)

* **Mt. Ordeals (undead):**
  * Palom: Fira on all with 2+ enemies, else the weakness spell.
  * Tellah: weakness spells on 3+ enemies while above 40 MP, else Defend (keeps 30 MP).
  * Porom: heals (the pilot's Cure, and Cure on undead), else Defends.
  * Cecil: attacks.
* **Milon:**
  * Palom: Fira on all.
  * Tellah: Fire on all.
  * Cecil: attacks Milon (route killFirst 166).
  * Porom: heals.
* **Milon Z:**
  * Palom: Fira (Fire when low).
  * Tellah: Fire.
  * Porom: heals.
  * Back attack: Row (automatic).
* **Dark Knight:** Cecil Defends (`whileMonster` 206). Scripted fights now use plan rules tied to a monster; before, they always attacked.
