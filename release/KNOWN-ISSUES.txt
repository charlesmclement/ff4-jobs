0.2.0 alpha candidate — known limits

A complete campaign and native Windows/clean-prefix installation have not
been certified. Keep backup saves and use a separate test slot.

Jobs use existing FF4 actions, not every FF5 mechanic. No new Dualcast,
Freelancer mastery inheritance, Ninja dual wield or custom
Monk barehand formula. The local playtest now shares story-earned summons
with personal Summoner ABP requirements; later-story acceptance is pending.
Stat modifiers are provisional additive values.

Borrowed Dragoon waits until Zot, but multiple party members can still
Jump afterward. No party-wide airborne limit is implemented. Encounter
balance, scripted battles and queued-action interactions need playtesting.

Native character identity, slot restrictions and FF4 combat calculations
remain. Equipment eligibility is changed by the main job; uncommon story,
Optimize, full-inventory and magnetic-cave interactions need campaign QA.

The English Jobs UI is not localized. Other gameplay/UI/save mods are
unverified. The plugin only enables on the recorded GameAssembly hash.

The isometric experiment, VSync changes and keyboard remaps are separate
and are not included. Development diagnostics require the explicit
FF4JOBS_TEST_MODE=1 environment variable; normal launches do not run them.

New local playtest: native command triangles and skill/spell unlock dialogs
are installed but await visual/controller acceptance by the tester.
