FF4 JOBS+ 0.4.1 beta 2 — PLAYTEST GUIDE

Beta 2 priority: newly joined Yang, remove/re-equip each claw, move between
hands, check inventory counts, then try Optimize. No crash or item loss.

You can continue an existing campaign. Keep your original backup; no new
game, all-jobs override or ABP reset is required. Story gates remain active.

PRIORITY CHECKS AS YOU PLAY
- Save/reload after changing a main/sub job or earning a new ability.
- Check returning characters and Cecil's transformation retain personal ABP.
- Monk: fists and claws should each produce two independently calculated
  strikes per normal Attack. Fists gain 2 attack over native Monk power. Claws
  retain native elemental/status effects. Check misses and first-hit kills.
- Red Mage: Dualcast at 310 ABP, two spell/target selections, one charge,
  two costs. Check cancelling, insufficient MP, Silence and auto-battle.
- Ranger: Rapid Fire after Zot, main 150/sub 310 ABP. Four half-damage
  strikes, choosing a living enemy each time; no extra Barehand strikes.
- Ninja: Dual Wield at main 70/sub 150 ABP. Check both hands, Optimize,
  inventory counts and switching to a main job with different equipment.
- Mastery: at 310 ABP the character keeps bounded stat benefits. Original
  inherits mastered passives; repeated switching must not inflate stats.
- Menus: A/B, directional repeat, command scrolling and triangle indicators.
- Story: job/spell announcements and gated skills must match JOBS.txt.

REPORTING
Report at https://github.com/charlesmclement/ff4-jobs/issues
Include mod/game/loader versions, character, main/sub job, ABP, location,
exact steps, expected/actual result, other mods and relevant BepInEx log.
Remove personal paths/account details. Share a backed-up save privately
only if needed. Do not upload game assets, executables or interop DLLs.

ACCEPTANCE SCOPE
Beta means the intended feature set is implemented and ready for campaign
playtesting. It does not mean a complete campaign or every combat combination
has been verified. Native Windows and clean-prefix installations remain
platform testing; the automated startup check covers the installed Proton
environment only. See KNOWN-ISSUES.txt and the repository validation notes.
